What is Managed IT and how is CES different?

Managed IT means outsourcing your daily IT responsibilities to a specialized provider. While most Managed Service Providers (MSPs) rely on ticket queues and tiered support levels, CES operates on an advanced enterprise model. We eliminate service delays by immediately assigning an experienced engineer to your issue instead of relying on a multi-tiered escalation process.

Do you offer remote or on-site support?

We provide both remote and on-site support depending on the nature of the issue. Most support requests can be resolved remotely within minutes by our helpdesk, but we also dispatch engineers on-site for hardware installations, physical networking issues, and hands-on troubleshooting when required.

How do you handle cybersecurity and compliance?

Cybersecurity is woven directly into the fabric of our strategic IT planning—not treated as an optional add-on. We implement proactive, multi-layered defenses, including zero-trust frameworks and persistent monitoring. We are highly experienced in supporting regulated industries and ensure strict technical alignment with compliance standards like HIPAA, PCI-DSS, FTC Safeguards, and CMMC to minimize risk and optimize your cyber insurance standing.
