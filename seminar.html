AI Integration

What AI Can and Can’t Do for Your Business. Attend our no-hype seminar designed to help business owners and CEOs understand how artificial intelligence can realistically improve operations, productivity, and decision-making while also clarifying its limitations. Attendees will leave with a clear understanding of where AI delivers measurable value, how to take the next steps toward AI implementation, and integration strategies so organizations can adopt AI responsibly and strategically.

Cybersecurity & Insurance

Cybersecurity Training & Hidden Insurance Cost Analysis. This session examines the growing intersection between AI adoption, cybersecurity risk, and cyber insurance requirements. CEOs will gain an in-depth understanding of cybersecurity as it relates specifically to their company’s operating environment, while also receiving expert insight from a licensed cybersecurity insurance agent on the risks associated with various security gaps and exposure areas. Attendees will leave with a clear understanding of how specific cybersecurity decisions directly impact organizational risk, liability, and insurance coverage.
